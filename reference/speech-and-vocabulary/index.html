Speech and vocabulary

The commands of the group “Speech and vocabulary”. The text of each command is the output of office help <command>.

models download

Section titled “models download”

download the speech model and the VAD model ahead of time.

models list

Section titled “models list”

list the speech models and the one that join would use.

vocabulary extract

Section titled “vocabulary extract”

write .office/vocabulary.txt from the documents of a project.
