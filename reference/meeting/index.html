Meeting

The commands of the group “Meeting”. The text of each command is the output of office help <command>.

hand raise

Section titled “hand raise”

raise your hand.

meeting end

Section titled “meeting end”

end the meeting for everyone.

meeting events

Section titled “meeting events”

read events after a cursor from the hub.

meeting join

Section titled “meeting join”

join a meeting, start the local log and print its path.

meeting leave

Section titled “meeting leave”

leave the meeting.

meeting log

Section titled “meeting log”

print the path of the local log.

meeting pause

Section titled “meeting pause”

pause your transcription.

meeting resume

Section titled “meeting resume”

resume your transcription.

meeting view

Section titled “meeting view”

gateway view of your own utterances.

minutes update

Section titled “minutes update”

write the running minutes of the meeting.

say

Section titled “say”

submit a typed utterance.

signal addressed

Section titled “signal addressed”

tell the meeting that a speaker addressed someone.

signal silence-long

Section titled “signal silence-long”

tell the meeting that it was silent for a long time.

signal turn-end

Section titled “signal turn-end”

tell the meeting that a turn has ended.

utterance off-the-record

Section titled “utterance off-the-record”

mark one of your sentences off the record.
